ABC FINTECH NBFC - DIGITAL LENDING SOP (INTERNAL POLICY)
Sample document with intentional compliance gaps for testing

1. PURPOSE
Govern digital personal loan origination via mobile app and web (up to INR 5 lakh).

2. ELIGIBILITY & ONBOARDING
PAN verification, Aadhaar OTP, bank statement upload, credit bureau pull required.

3. LOAN APPLICATION WORKFLOW
Select amount/tenure -> credit scoring -> offer with rate and EMI -> OTP accept ->
e-sign agreement -> disbursement within 24 hours.

4. INTEREST & FEES
Interest 18%-36% p.a. Processing fee up to 3%. Late penalty 2% per month on overdue EMI.

5. COLLECTIONS
EMI via UPI auto-pay if customer opts in. Field collection after 60 DPD.

6. PARTNER INTEGRATION
Leads from digital marketplace partners via API. Commission per disbursed loan.

7. IT SECURITY
AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2 in transit. Authorized staff access. Annual VAPT.

8. ROLES
Product, Operations, Risk, Compliance quarterly review.

NOTE: This SOP intentionally omits KFS, cooling-off, grievance redressal, data retention,
APR, LSP oversight, direct disbursement, explicit auto-debit consent, Video KYC, and FPC.
