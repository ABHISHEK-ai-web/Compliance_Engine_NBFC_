RBI DIGITAL LENDING GUIDELINES (2022) - SAMPLE CIRCULAR
Reserve Bank of India | For Compliance Engine Testing

SECTION 1 - INTRODUCTION
Regulated entities (REs) and Lending Service Providers (LSPs) must ensure transparent,
fair, and borrower-centric digital lending practices.

SECTION 3.2 - KEY FACT STATEMENT (KFS)
REs shall provide a Key Fact Statement (KFS) to the borrower in standardized format
before execution of the loan contract. Borrower acknowledgment of KFS must be obtained
prior to disbursement.

SECTION 3.4 - TRANSPARENT PRICING
The all-inclusive Annual Percentage Rate (APR) must be disclosed prominently.
Total cost of borrowing including all fees and charges must be stated clearly.

SECTION 4.1 - COOLING-OFF / LOOK-UP PERIOD
Borrowers must be given a cooling-off period (minimum 3 days) or look-up period
during which they may exit the digital loan without penalty.

SECTION 4.3 - DISBURSEMENT
Loan amount shall be disbursed directly to the borrower's bank account only.

SECTION 4.5 - AUTO-DEBIT / REPAYMENT
Setting up auto-debit or e-mandate/NACH requires explicit consent from the borrower.

SECTION 5.3 - DATA PRIVACY
Data minimization, purpose limitation, need-to-know access, data retention and
data deletion policies are mandatory.

SECTION 2.2 - LSP OVERSIGHT
RE remains responsible for LSP actions. LSP oversight and third party oversight required.

SECTION 6 - GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Grievance redressal mechanism with complaint mechanism and escalation matrix required.

KYC - VIDEO KYC (V-CIP)
Video KYC and video-based identification for remote onboarding per RBI KYC Directions.

FAIR PRACTICES CODE
Regulated entities must adopt and publish Fair Practices Code (FPC).
